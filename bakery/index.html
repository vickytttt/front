Our Story

A family bakery with a big love for baking

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What hours are you open?

We're open daily from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and until 8 PM on weekends.

Do you take custom cake orders?

Yes! Please give us at least 48 hours' notice and reach out through the contact form.

Are your products suitable for vegetarians?

Most of our items are vegetarian and made with dairy. Please mention any allergies when ordering.

Do you offer delivery?

We offer local pickup and same-day delivery within 10 km for orders over $40.
